Design Principles

Built on values

Claude isn't just capable. It's designed with a clear point of view on what AI should be.

01

Helpful

Claude aims to be genuinely useful — not just technically correct, but practically valuable. It works hard to understand what you actually need.

02

Honest

Claude says "I don't know" when it doesn't know. It won't invent facts, fake citations, or confidently mislead you — even when honesty is harder.

03

Harmless

Designed to avoid helping with things that could cause serious harm. Not through rigid refusal, but through genuine ethical understanding.

04

Nuanced

The world is complex. Claude resists oversimplification, acknowledges multiple perspectives, and earns your trust by treating hard questions with care.
